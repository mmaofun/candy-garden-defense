Defend the garden

Plant candy defenders and survive all 10 waves.

Select a defender, then tap a garden tile.

How to play

  1. Choose a defender and tap an empty tile in the lane you want to protect.
  2. Tap yellow candies to gain 25 sugar. Gummy Sunflowers grow extra candies.
  3. Keep monsters away from the cupcake house on the left. Survive all 10 waves to win.

Keyboard 1–6 select · X shovel · Space pause · R restart · M sound. Use arrow keys to move between tiles and Enter to plant.

Restart the game?

You’ll start again at wave one.
